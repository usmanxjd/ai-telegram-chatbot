# Customer Support FAQ

## Refund policy
Customers can request a refund within 14 days of purchase if usage is below 20% of allocated credits.

## Delivery delays
If order delivery is delayed more than 48 hours beyond ETA, offer expedited replacement or full refund.

## Account access
Users who forgot passwords should use the reset link and contact support if they do not receive email in 10 minutes.

## Human escalation
For billing disputes, fraud concerns, or legal requests, escalate to a human support specialist immediately.
